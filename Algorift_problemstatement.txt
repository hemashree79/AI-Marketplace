Application Description & Functionality: 
Our project, "AI Marketplace", is a dedicated e-commerce platform built specifically for AI models.

Role-Based Access: The website supports three distinct login types: Admin, Developer, and User.
Developer Tools: Developers can easily register and upload their AI products, providing details such as the AI logo, technical specifications, and the price.
Quality Control: To make sure the platform remains safe and trustworthy, an Admin must review and approve every product before it goes live.
Seamless Purchasing: Once approved, regular users can browse products on the homepage. Clicking on a model reveals all developer details and specs, allowing users to purchase immediately using the "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" buttons.
Solution to the Problem Statement: The AI ecosystem currently makes it difficult for everyday users to find and trust AI models, while developers struggle to easily sell their work. Our AI Marketplace directly solves this by creating a transparent, central platform that brings creators and users together safely.

Simplified Discovery: We designed a familiar, intuitive e-commerce interface so that discovering, evaluating, and buying AI models is as easy as everyday online shopping.
Trust & Verification: We implemented a strict Admin approval system. This vital step ensures that only safe, working models reach the public, establishing user confidence.
Direct Monetization: We solved the monetization problem by allowing developers to set their own prices, providing users with clear "Add to Cart" and "Buy Now" checkout options.
Clear Structure: By having Developers build, Admins verify, and Users buy, our solution simplifies the entire lifecycle of how people adopt AI technology. It removes technical confusion and provides a clean, trustworthy store for AI tools.
